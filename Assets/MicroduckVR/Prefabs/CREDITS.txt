MICRODUCK LAB - ITEM PACK
=========================

Toys, furniture, household clutter and the Microduck robot from the game Microduck Lab
(https://github.com/R1chi33333/microduck-lab), as separate Unity prefabs.

  Assets/MicroduckVR/Prefabs/Toys        the toy-bag toys (physics, collider, beak-grabbable)
  Assets/MicroduckVR/Prefabs/Furniture   sofa, tables, chairs, bed, kitchen units, shelves, ...
  Assets/MicroduckVR/Prefabs/Clutter     books, mugs, plushies, shoes, plants, boxes, ...
  Assets/MicroduckVR/Prefabs/Ducks       the Microduck robot in four colourways  (NON-COMMERCIAL, see below)
  Assets/MicroduckVR/Prefabs/Meshes      one mesh file per item
  Assets/MicroduckVR/Materials, Shaders, Textures   the comic toon materials and shaders they use

USING THE ITEMS
---------------

- Unity 6 with the Universal Render Pipeline (URP).
- Units are metres; each item's pivot is at its bottom centre (where it touches the floor), unrotated.
- The prefabs keep the game's layer numbers: World 6 (furniture), Duck 7, Toy 8 (beak-grabbable toys),
  Prop 10 (loose props). Name those layers in your project (Project Settings > Tags and Layers) or set your own.
- Loose props and toys have a Rigidbody and one simple collider; Grabbable marks things a duck can carry
  in its beak; SurfaceTag tells the game what a surface is made of. Both scripts work on their own.
- Ink outlines: add a "Full Screen Pass Renderer Feature" to your URP renderer with the pass material
  Assets/MicroduckVR/Materials/ComicOutline.mat (requirements: Depth and Normal) for the black comic lines.
  Without it the items still show their flat toon colours.
